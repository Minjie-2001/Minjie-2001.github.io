PROJECT 01 · FUNCTIONAL YARNS

Multifunctional Core-Shell
Sensing Yarn

Development of a multifunctional core-shell composite yarn integrating flexible strain sensing with antibacterial, photothermal, hemostatic, and drug-release functionalities for wearable and biomedical textile applications.

Core-Shell Yarn Flexible Sensing Electrospinning Biofunctionality
01

OVERVIEW

Integrating flexible sensing and biofunctionality within a single yarn.

Conventional conductive sensing yarns are primarily designed for motion sensing, while the integration of biological functions such as antibacterial activity, hemostasis, and controlled drug release remains challenging.

This project developed a multifunctional core-shell composite yarn by integrating a conductive spandex/MXene/AgNW sensing core with an electrospun collagen nanofibrous shell. UV crosslinking and a metal-phenolic network (MPN) were further introduced to stabilize and functionalize the collagen layer.

The resulting SMACM (spandex/MXene/AgNW/Collagen/MPN) yarn combines strain sensing with biocompatibility, antibacterial activity, photothermal performance, hemostatic capability, and sustained drug release, providing a multifunctional platform for wearable sensing and bioactive textile applications.

STRUCTURAL DESIGN & FABRICATION

A hierarchical core-shell architecture for sensing and biofunctional integration.

The composite yarn was constructed through a sequential functionalization strategy. A conductive sensing core was first established on an elastic spandex substrate, followed by electrospinning of a collagen nanofibrous shell and subsequent biofunctional modification.

PERFORMANCE & APPLICATIONS

From flexible sensing to multifunctional bioactivity.

The core–shell architecture enables the integration of reliable strain sensing with multiple biofunctional properties within a single yarn platform, demonstrating potential for wearable sensing and bioactive textile applications.

01

Strain Sensing

The MXene/AgNW conductive network provides reliable strain sensing and enables the monitoring of human motions including finger, wrist, and knee bending.

02

Antibacterial Activity

MPN functionalization provides strong antibacterial activity against both S. aureus and E. coli, with inhibition rates reaching 88.8% and 95.5%, respectively.

03

Photothermal Performance

The synergistic contributions of MXene, AgNW, and the metal–phenolic network provide effective photothermal conversion and further enhance the multifunctionality of the yarn.

04

Biocompatibility

CCK-8 evaluation confirmed good cytocompatibility, supporting the potential use of the composite yarn in biointerface and wearable applications.

05

Hemostasis & Absorption

The collagen nanofibrous shell enhances water and blood absorption and contributes to blood clotting, providing the yarn with additional hemostatic functionality.

06

Sustained Drug Release

The biofunctional shell can serve as a drug-loading interface and exhibits sustained release behavior, further extending the potential of the yarn toward bioactive textile applications.
