PROJECT 02 · MULTIFUNCTIONAL AEROGELS

Multifunctional Collagen/Cellulose
Nanofiber Aerogels

Development of tannic acid-mediated collagen/cellulose nanofiber composite aerogels through regulation of collagen self-assembly and supramolecular double-network construction for multifunctional wound dressing applications.

Collagen Cellulose Nanofibers Self-Assembly Aerogels Wound Dressing
01

OVERVIEW

Regulating collagen self-assembly to construct multifunctional supramolecular aerogels.

Modern wound dressings require the integration of mechanical stability, antibacterial activity, hemostasis, biocompatibility, and controlled drug release within a single material platform.

This project developed a multifunctional collagen/cellulose nanofiber composite aerogel using tannic acid to regulate collagen self-assembly. Collagen fibrils and cellulose nanofibers form a supramolecular double-network structure, while tannic acid further strengthens intermolecular interactions and promotes collagen fibril formation.

The resulting COL/CNF@TA aerogel combines enhanced structural stability with sustained drug release, antibacterial activity, good biocompatibility, and effective hemostatic performance, demonstrating potential as a multifunctional wound dressing.

MATERIAL DESIGN & FABRICATION

Building a supramolecular double network through collagen self-assembly regulation.

The composite aerogel was constructed by integrating collagen, cellulose nanofibers, and tannic acid. Tannic acid regulates collagen self-assembly and strengthens intermolecular interactions, promoting the formation of a stable supramolecular double-network structure.

Structural design and multifunctional applications of the tannic acid-mediated COL/CNF composite aerogel.

PERFORMANCE & APPLICATIONS

Combining structural stability with multifunctional wound-care performance.

The tannic acid-regulated supramolecular double-network architecture provides the composite aerogel with enhanced structural stability while integrating sustained drug release, antibacterial activity, biocompatibility, absorption, and hemostatic performance.

01

Mechanical Reinforcement

The synergistic reinforcement of CNFs and tannic acid significantly improves structural stability, with the optimized COL/CNF@TA aerogel reaching a peak compressive strength of 26.10 kPa.

02

Sustained Drug Release

The supramolecular double-network structure effectively regulates the diffusion of 5-FU and provides pronounced sustained-release behavior compared with the pure collagen aerogel.

03

Antibacterial Activity

Tannic acid introduces antibacterial functionality, enabling the composite aerogel to inhibit both E. coli and S. aureus.

04

Biocompatibility

Cell viability evaluation demonstrates good cytocompatibility and biological activity, supporting potential biomedical applications.

05

Hemostatic Performance

The porous supramolecular network promotes blood-cell attachment and coagulation, with composite aerogels exhibiting blood clotting index values below 10%.

06

Wound Dressing

Strong water and blood absorption, together with antibacterial, hemostatic, and drug-release functions, demonstrates potential for multifunctional wound dressing applications.
