About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Materials Science and Engineering at TIANGONG University. My research focuses on bio-based functional materials, with particular interests in cellulose-based materials, flexible sensing, and wound dressings.

Born in 2000 in Sichuan Province, China, I developed a strong interest in science and engineering and have pursued my academic journey in materials and textile-related research.

Outside research, I enjoy Japanese anime and video games. I also enjoy exploring new technologies and learning about developments beyond my own research field.

Research Projects

Multifunctional Core-Shell Sensing Yarn

A core-shell composite yarn integrating a spandex/MXene/AgNW conductive sensing core with an electrospun collagen biofunctional shell for flexible sensing and multifunctional biomedical applications.

Core-Shell Yarn Flexible Sensing Electrospinning Biofunctionality

02

Multifunctional Collagen/Cellulose Nanofiber Aerogels

Development of tannic acid-mediated collagen/cellulose nanofiber composite aerogels through regulation of collagen self-assembly and supramolecular double-network construction for sustained drug release, antibacterial activity, hemostasis, and multifunctional wound dressing applications.

Collagen Cellulose Nanofibers Aerogels Wound Dressing
→
03

Pigskin Collagen Extraction & Wet-Spun Fibers

Optimization of ultrasound-assisted enzymatic extraction of type I collagen from pigskin using response surface methodology, followed by the fabrication of collagen fibers through wet spinning and evaluation of their structural and mechanical properties.

Collagen Ultrasound-Assisted Extraction Wet Spinning Bio-based Fibers
